Passive Network Detection & Response — product datasheet

CodeRed NDR is a passive network detection and response sensor. It receives a copy of network traffic via a SPAN or TAP port, analyses it in real time across three complementary detection engines, and forwards enriched alerts to your SIEM — no inline deployment, no agents, no impact on production traffic.

Threat coverage

C2 & covert channels

JA3/JA3S TLS fingerprinting (Cobalt Strike, Sliver, Brute Ratel, Havoc, AsyncRAT), beaconing, domain fronting, DGA & DNS tunneling, ICMP tunneling, Tor.

Lateral movement

SMB admin-share & Pass-the-Hash, RDP brute force/spray, WMI & WinRM/PsExec, SSH HASSH fingerprinting (Impacket, Paramiko).

Credential attacks

Kerberoasting, AS-REP roasting, Golden/Silver ticket, LLMNR/NBT-NS poisoning (Responder), NTLM relay, LDAP AD recon.

Exfiltration & impact

Ransomware mass-write & shadow-copy deletion, double-extortion exfil, insider data staging, FTP/email bulk exfil, long & asymmetric connections.

Commodity threats

Cryptomining (Stratum, pool domains, XMRig), port scans & sweeps, certificate anomalies (self-signed, short-validity, IP-as-CN).

OT/ICS & cloud

Industrial protocol attacks (Modbus, DNP3) and cloud-specific threats — IMDS abuse, AWS key exposure, cloud-storage C2.

Deployment

  • On-prem: VMware ESXi / vSphere via SPAN or hardware TAP
  • Cloud: AWS, Azure, GCP, Alibaba Cloud (traffic-mirroring mode)
  • Single- or multi-NIC; toggle cloud mode post-deploy
  • Guided setup wizard — first alert in the SIEM within minutes

SIEM integration

Validated with Wazuh, Pre Security, QRadar, ArcSight, Graylog, and Splunk. Full log detail (DNS, HTTP, TLS, IDS alerts, ML anomalies) plus Netflow metadata, encrypted in transit.
